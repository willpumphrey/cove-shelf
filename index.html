Pick the McKesson order or invoice export (.xls, .xlsx or .csv) from Files. Items land on the shelf flagged “needs lot/exp scan”; then scan each box to fill lot and expiration.

On iPhone, Scan with camera opens the normal camera, takes one photo of the barcode, and reads it. Live scan is the backup. If either misses, type the numbers under the bars. Expiration is still read off the box unless the code itself contains it.

Same barcode with the same lot and expiration adds to quantity. A different lot or date becomes its own row. Medications and controlled substances stay in AtlasMD, not here.
